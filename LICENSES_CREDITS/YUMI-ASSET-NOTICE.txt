Yumi avatar asset notice

Public installers do **not** include the yumi Live2D model, textures,
motions, or expressions. Cubism Core may ship under
LIVE2D_PUBLICATION_LICENSE.json; character models are user-imported
during onboarding.

A local `皮套-yumi` / `Live-2D-yumi` folder may still exist in the
developer workspace for fixtures. Setting REVERIE_YUMI_SOURCE in
development can load it for the current user profile only. That path
is never staged into a public NSIS payload.
