Focus soundscape asset notice (bundled ambient recordings)

The DreamRoom companion soundscape ships three real environmental
recordings, trimmed into seamless loops (2s triangular wrap crossfade) and
loudness-normalised (EBU R128, I=-23 LUFS / TP=-2 dB) with FFmpeg. The
processed files are the ones bundled; SHA-256 below refers to the processed
assets in frontend/src/assets/dreamroom/.
