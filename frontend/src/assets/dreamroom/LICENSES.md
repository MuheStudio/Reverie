# DreamRoom asset licence

`ink-calm.svg`, `ink-warm.svg`, `ink-rain.svg`, and `paper-grain.svg` are
original procedural vector assets created for Reverie in July 2026.

They are distributed under the same GPL-3.0 licence as the Reverie frontend.
They contain no third-party artwork, fonts, model data, or remote resources.

`focus-rain.wav`, `focus-fire.wav`, and `focus-library.wav` are original,
deterministically generated mono PCM loops created for Reverie in July 2026.
Their periodic synthesis makes the loop boundary continuous; they do not
contain recordings, samples, streamed media, or other third-party material.
The generator source is retained as `.generate-focus-sounds.cjs` beside the
assets so the provenance and exact output remain auditable.
