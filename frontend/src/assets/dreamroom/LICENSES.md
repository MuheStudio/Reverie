# DreamRoom asset licence

`ink-calm.svg`, `ink-warm.svg`, `ink-rain.svg`, and `paper-grain.svg` are
original procedural vector assets created for Reverie in July 2026.

`rug.svg`, `plush-toys.svg`, `balloon.svg`, `star-lantern.svg`, and
`star-wire.svg` are original hand-drawn vector assets created for Reverie in
August 2026 (DreamRoom decoration batch). They are distributed under the same
GPL-3.0 licence as the Reverie frontend.
They contain no third-party artwork, fonts, model data, or remote resources.

`focus-rain.ogg`, `focus-fire.ogg`, and `focus-wind.ogg` are real
environmental recordings (August 2026), trimmed into seamless loops and
loudness-normalised. Their sources, licences, and attributions are recorded
in `LICENSES_CREDITS/FOCUS-SOUNDS-NOTICE.txt` at the repository root:

- `focus-rain.ogg` — "AMB Rain Loop 2" by Kresiek The Furry, CC0
  (https://opengameart.org/content/amb-rain-loop-2)
- `focus-fire.ogg` — "Fireplace Sound Loop" (fire.wav), CC0
  (https://opengameart.org/content/fireplace-sound-loop)
- `focus-wind.ogg` — "Wind Loop", adapted from a recording by Jonathan Shaw
  (InspectorJ), CC-BY 3.0 — attribution required
  (https://opengameart.org/content/wind-loop)

`focus-library.wav` remains an original, deterministically generated mono PCM
loop created for Reverie in July 2026. Its periodic synthesis makes the loop
boundary continuous; it does not contain recordings, samples, streamed media,
or other third-party material. The generator source is retained as
`.generate-focus-sounds.cjs` beside the assets so the provenance and exact
output remain auditable.
